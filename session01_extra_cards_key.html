Card A · build it from the steps · about 15 minutes

Silly story

animal = input("Name an animal: ")
food = input("Name a food: ")
place = input("Name a place: ")
number = input("Pick a whole number: ")
number = int(number)
double = number * 2

print(f"One day the {animal} walked into {place}.")
print(f"It ordered {number} plates of {food}.")
print(f"Then it changed its mind and ordered {double}.")

What it prints

Name an animal: penguin
Name a food: tacos
Name a place: the library
Pick a whole number: 7
One day the penguin walked into the library.
It ordered 7 plates of tacos.
Then it changed its mind and ordered 14.

Try it: the program without its int() line, run with 7.

The last line is Then it changed its mind and ordered 77. Text times 2 is the same text written twice.

Card B · build it from the steps · about 15 minutes

Age calculator

name = input("Your name: ")
age = input("Your age in years: ")
age = int(age)

days = age * 365
hours = days * 24
later = age + 10

print(f"{name}, you are about {days} days old.")
print(f"That is about {hours} hours.")
print(f"In 10 years you will be {later}.")

What it prints

Your name: Ada
Your age in years: 11
Ada, you are about 4015 days old.
That is about 96360 hours.
In 10 years you will be 21.

Before you run: with the two lines the other way round it stops with NameError: name 'days' is not defined

Go further: minutes = hours * 60 and seconds = minutes * 60, each with its own print line. For age 11 they print 5781600 and 346896000.
